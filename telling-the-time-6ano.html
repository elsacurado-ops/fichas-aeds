⏰ Telling the Time

What time is it? Learn, practise and check your answers!

💡 Remember!

  • :00 → o'clock  (7:00 = seven o'clock)
  • :15 → quarter past  (7:15 = quarter past seven)
  • :30 → half past  (7:30 = half past seven)
  • :45 → quarter to + next hour  (7:45 = quarter to eight)
  • Minutes 1 to 30 → PAST the hour
  • Minutes 31 to 59 → TO the next hour
